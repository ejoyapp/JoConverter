**JoConverter** is a professional, high-performance desktop application for media conversion, timeline editing, and video processing. Designed for creators and professionals, it provides a seamless and secure environment to edit, enhance, and convert your media files with uncompromising speed.

> **Note:** This repository is used exclusively for hosting version update configurations, release notes, and update manifests for the JoConverter desktop client. JoConverter is a commercial software product, and the core source code is not public.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎞️ Intuitive Video Editing
* **Non-Linear Timeline:** Edit your videos with a powerful, multi-track timeline supporting video, audio, and subtitle layers.
* **Real-Time Preview:** Instantly preview your edits, cuts, and transitions without waiting for pre-rendering.
* **A/B Media Comparison:** Professional split-screen and side-by-side comparison tools to evaluate video quality and enhancements in real time.

### ⚡ High-Performance Media Engine
* **Universal Format Support:** Powered by an advanced internal engine, JoConverter supports importing and exporting almost all modern audio and video formats.
* **Lightning-Fast Conversion:** Optimized multi-stage rendering pipelines designed to maximize your CPU and hardware acceleration capabilities.
* **Smart Audio Processing:** Built-in audio extraction, precise waveform generation, and synchronization tools.

### 🔒 Secure & Private
* **100% Local Processing:** Your media never leaves your computer. All rendering, editing, and conversion happen entirely offline on your local machine.
* **Hardware-Bound Licensing:** A seamless, secure, and privacy-respecting commercial activation system ensures your Pro license is safely tied to your device without requiring a constant internet connection.
